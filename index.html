Biggest event handled

Tri Mandir Opening, Pune

A 40-acre site built out over two months — temporary structures, staging, power, crowd flow and hospitality — opening to 45,000 attendees across the ceremony.

45k+Attendees
₹1.5Cr+Budget · ~US$180K
2 monthsProduction time
100+Manpower & team
20+Vendors

The constraint

Two months from first site visit to doors, sharing the ground with ongoing construction. Everything we put up had to be temporary, and ceremony crowds arrive in waves rather than to a schedule, so the site had to absorb a surge without a queue backing up onto the approach road.

What I did

Split the site into build, congregation, hospitality and parking zones so each could be handed over on its own date instead of waiting on one critical path. Ran 20+ vendors and 100+ crew against a single master schedule, and kept the temple precinct clear of production traffic until the final week.

Outcome

45,000+ attendees across the day, delivered to the two-month schedule and inside a ₹1.5Cr+ budget — the largest crowd, venue and budget I have been responsible for.
